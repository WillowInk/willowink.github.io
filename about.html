~About Willow Ink~

Who we are:

Willow ink is a one person illustration studio emerging in the game market. I handle everything, from the sprites, to the programming to the advertisements and the wince worthy writing on this website. If there is a skill I don't know, I seek to learn it. The idea is that without a team of people that might certainly make things quicker or have an extended background in the chosen field, I possess only my own thoughts to bicker ideas with and have full creative freedom.

At this moment in time, Willow ink currently resides in South Korea (KST/UTC +9)

Our goal:

The goal behind Willow ink is entertainment and in between all of the lines of code and all the frames drawn, there is love and hope contained in every pixel. Love in a sense of respect to the player, whom may have strong preference for games that take an exorbitant amount of time and care in ensuring a fantastic experience and wishes not to waste their time or money. And hope, hope that people genuinely enjoys the effort, time and stories enough to bring a big goofy smile to their face. And perhaps history or philosphy appreciation here and there.

What we do:

Willow ink is proficient in 2D illustrations and Pixel illustrations/animations. All sprites, backgrounds and programming is made by Willow Ink and Willow Ink alone. Any departure from this will be credited accordingly. My ambitions are front end code with natural fluency, GD Script without a thousand bugs or crying about thinking about a thousand bugs and perhaps a warm audience to love my stories and make embarrassing memes out of. Jokes aside, the equipment available to me is an ipad 10 gifted by a friend, an average PC and 3-4 cups of coffee a day. Don't try this at home kids.

What we are (and are not) interested in:

Due to the nature of one of the future games that is being planned (A game involving English history) we currently strongly welcome a UK based audience and any suggestions, simple inquires, and comments are strongly encouraged. Willow Ink takes a strong interest in historic folklore in general so any folklore from all countries are welcome and I will always give you a little heads up if I want to use the information you so very generously may give to put in any of our games and credit accordingly. Keep in mind I will very thoroughly research and cross reference any information given and deliberate malice or lying will not be appreciated and I may stop contact without warning depending on the severity. Any fanart is very very appreciated and will go into the Gallery post haste!

The Willow Ink brand does not wish to associate with any of the following:

  • Dangerously explicit adult content,
  • Extreme political messaging,
  • Harrassment of any kind (Demands, inappropriate behavior, spam, etc.),
  • Blatant disrespect or lack of decorum,
  • Accusations, especially those aimed to slander

If recieved, I may cease contact and may not respond. Let us respect eachothers time and not burn our beautiful daylight on malice.

Regarding the use of AI

Willow Ink does not use any generative ai in our games and especially our illustrations. Aside from there being absolutely no need other than perhaps saving time, every frame, pixel, line of code is learned through asking the most foolish of questions, tears, patience and a bad back from folding over my desk like a cooked shrimp. Allow me to clarify, it isn't a boast and certainly not a box to tick for acceptance. The most straightforward answer is that there simply is no need, and therefore I do not use it. I am willing to put in the time and learn so that the skills stay with me. This also allows complete control over my creative decisions. This section is here purely for the people this matters to.
