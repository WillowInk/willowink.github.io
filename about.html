~About Willow Ink~

Who we are:

Willow Ink is a one person illustration studio emerging in the game market. I handle everything, from the sprites, to the programming to the advertisements and the wince worthy writing on this website. If there is a skill I don't know, I seek to learn it. The idea is that without a team of people that might certainly make things quicker or have an extended background in the chosen field, I possess only my own thoughts to bicker ideas with and have full creative freedom.

At this moment in time, Willow ink currently resides in South Korea (KST/UTC +9)

Our goal:

The goal behind Willow Ink is entertainment and in between all of the lines of code and all the frames drawn, there is love and hope contained in every pixel. Love in a sense of respect to the player, whom may have strong preference for games that take an exorbitant amount of time and care in ensuring a fantastic experience and wishes not to waste their time or money. And hope, hope that people genuinely enjoy the effort, time and stories enough to bring a big goofy smile to their face. And perhaps history or philosiphy appreciation here and there.

What we do:

Willow ink is proficient in 2D illustrations and Pixel illustrations/animations. All sprites, backgrounds and programming is made by Willow Ink and Willow Ink alone. Any departure from this will be credited accordingly. My ambitions are front end code with natural fluency, GD Script without a thousand bugs or crying about thinking about a thousand bugs and perhaps a warm audience to love my stories and make embarrassing memes out of. Jokes aside, the equipment available to me is an ipad 10 gifted by a friend, an average PC and 3-4 cups of coffee a day. Don't try this at home kids.

What we are (and are not) interested in:

Due to the nature of one of the future games that is being planned (A game involving English history) we currently strongly welcome a UK based audience and any suggestions, simple inquiries, and comments are strongly encouraged. Willow Ink takes a strong interest in historic folklore in general so any folklore from all countries are welcome and I will always give you a little heads up if I want to use the information you so very generously may give to put in any of our games and credit accordingly. Keep in mind I will very thoroughly research and cross reference any information given and deliberate malice or lying will not be appreciated and I may stop contact without warning depending on the severity. Any fanart is very very appreciated and will go into the Gallery post haste!

I want Willow Ink to be a kind and respectful place so I want to keep things simple: I engage with people who are courteous and genuine, and I step back from interactions that turn hostile, dishonest, or inappropriate. Life is too short to burn daylight on malice.

Regarding the use of AI

Willow Ink does not use any generative ai in our games and especially our illustrations. Every frame, pixel, line of code is learned through asking the most foolish of questions, tears, patience and a bad back from folding over my desk like a cooked shrimp. It's partly for creative control, and partly because I want the skills to be genuinely mine. This note is here for the people it matters to.
